System Modules

Collisions

Store collision date, time, severity, weather, road surface, lighting, and notes.

Vehicles

Record the vehicles involved, including type, make, model, registration, and speed.

Drivers

Manage driver details such as gender, age group, licence type, and impairment flag.

Casualties

Track casualty type, age group, gender, and injury severity for each collision.

Locations

Store road, district, junction, deprivation score, and speed limit information.

Interventions

Record safety measures such as speed cameras, signage, and junction redesigns.
